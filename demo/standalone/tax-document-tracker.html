Presenter Guide

Eight steps, about ten minutes. Everything on the right is live — drag the files yourself.

1 Set the scene

"Every tax season starts the same way: we send you a list of documents we need, and then everyone spends February playing email ping-pong. We replaced that with a shared folder that checks the documents itself."

Every request below starts at Missing. That's a brand-new engagement.

2 Pick the return type

"Setting up a new client takes a minute, not a morning. The first question is simply: what kind of return? A 1040 asks for W-2s and 1098s; an 1120-S asks for the trial balance and shareholder basis."

Click New Engagement and show the six form types.

3 Build the tailored list

"Here's the checklist from years of engagements. I tick what applies to your situation and the client's entire folder structure builds itself."

Name it after the prospect's own family or company. Untick a couple of rows, then create it.

4 What the client sees

"The client gets one shared folder — one folder per document, plus plain-English instructions. No portal, no password to forget. It's just a folder."

Click Client folder and read the _README.txt aloud.

5 Play the client

"Let's be an honest client: helpful, busy, and occasionally wrong. Both spouses' W-2s — plus last year's by mistake, the same one twice, a Word file where we need the 1098, and a vacation photo in the wrong place."

Drag every file from the tray into a row. Put vacation photo.jpg in the shared-folder root at the bottom.

6 The moment — scan

"In production this runs by itself every fifteen minutes. Watch what it worked out — without a person opening a single file."

Click Run Scan, then read the notes column out loud. That wording is what you forward to the client.

7 The accountant's view

"And it lands in the tool accountants already live in — Excel. Status, date received, plain-English notes, and an override column where your judgment beats any rule."

Click Manifest and show both sheets.

8 The trust close

"Three guarantees: every decision is a deterministic rule — no AI ever reads your tax documents. The scanner is read-only — it never moves, renames, or deletes a file. And your judgment always wins."

Point at the four assurances at the bottom of the screen.

Works the same on OneDrive or Google Drive — switch providers in the top bar to show a prospect their own setup.
