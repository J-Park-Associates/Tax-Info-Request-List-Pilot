Presenter Guide

Eight steps, about ten minutes. Everything on the right is live — drag the files yourself.

1 Set the scene

"Every tax season starts the same way: we send you a list of documents we need, and then everyone spends February playing email ping-pong. We replaced that with one shared folder that sorts itself."

Every request below starts at Missing. That's a brand-new engagement.

2 Pick the return type

"Setting up a new client takes a minute, not a morning. The first question is simply: what kind of return? A 1040 asks for W-2s and 1098s; an 1120-S asks for the trial balance and shareholder basis."

Click New Engagement and show the six form types.

3 Build the tailored list

"Here's the checklist from years of engagements. I tick what applies to your situation, and everything behind the scenes builds itself."

Name it after the prospect's own family or company. Untick a couple of rows, then create it.

4 What the client sees

"Here's the part your clients actually notice. They get one folder. Not a folder per document, not a portal with another password. The instruction is: drop everything in here. They never sort anything, never rename anything, never work out which file matches which line on our list."

Click Client folder and read a line of the README aloud.

5 Play the client

"Let's be an honest client: helpful, busy, and occasionally wrong. Both spouses' W-2s — plus last year's by mistake, the same one twice, a Word file where we need the 1098, and a vacation photo."

Drag the whole tray into the one drop folder. Don't sort it. The pile is the point — click Drop all of it in if you'd rather do it in one go.

6 The moment — sort and scan

"In production this runs by itself every fifteen minutes. Watch it take that pile apart — without a person opening a single file."

Click Sort & Scan. The drop folder empties, every original lands in PBC under the client's own name, and the renamed copies appear against each request.

7 The part that sells it

"Four things it refused to guess about. Read that first note: looks like A01, but the period is wrong — that's last year's W-2. It would have been easy to drop it into the prior-year returns folder. It didn't."

Walk Needs review, then open Index — every file the client sent, where it went, what we renamed it to.

8 The trust close

"Three guarantees. Every decision is a deterministic rule — no AI ever reads your tax documents. Your originals are never altered: we move them into PBC byte for byte under your own file names and work from copies. And your judgment always wins."

Point at the four assurances at the bottom of this screen.

Works the same on OneDrive or Google Drive — switch providers in the top bar to show a prospect their own setup.
