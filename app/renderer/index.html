Tax Document Tracker
Marketing Demo

Presenter Guide

Full script: DEMO-SCRIPT.md in the project folder.

1 Set the scene

"Every tax season starts the same way: we send you a list of documents we need, and then everyone spends February playing email ping-pong. We replaced that with a shared folder that checks the documents itself."

Click Reset Demo so every request shows Missing.

2 Pick the return type

"Setting up a new client takes a minute, not a morning. The first question is simply: what kind of return? Each form type carries its own tailored request list — a 1040 asks for W-2s and 1098s, an 1120-S asks for the trial balance and shareholder basis."

Click New Engagement. Show the form-type cards — 1040, 1120, 1120-S, 1065, 1041, 990 — then pick Form 1040.

3 Build the tailored list

"Here's the 1040 checklist from years of engagements — I tick what applies to your situation, add anything unusual, and the client's entire folder structure builds itself."

Name it after the prospect's own family or company. Untick a couple of items, add one custom request (e.g. "Rental Property Records"). Click Create Engagement, then Open Client Folder — the folders already exist.

4 What the client sees

"The client gets one shared folder — one folder per document, plus plain-English instructions. No portal, no logins to forget. It's just... a folder."

Click Open Client Folder. Show the folders and _README.txt.

5 Play the client

"Let's be the client. They drag files in — including, as clients do, last year's W-2 instead of this year's, a Word file where we need a PDF, a duplicate, and a vacation photo dropped in the wrong place."

Click Open Sample Documents. Drag into the client folder: all four W-2 PDFs (both spouses, the duplicate, and the 2024 one) → A01 · 1099-INT + 1099-DIV → A02 · 2024 Form 1040 Tax Return → B01 · Form 1098 + Mortgage Notes.docx → C01 · Donation Receipts → D01 · vacation photo.jpg → loose in the top folder.

6 The moment — scan

"In production this runs automatically every 15 minutes. Watch what it figured out — without any person opening a single file."

Click Run Scan. Walk the results: A01 Received — both 2025 W-2s found, the duplicate ignored, and the 2024 W-2 flagged in the notes. A02 Partial — 2 of 3, it knows exactly what's still owed. C01 — received the 1098, rejected the Word file. The photo shows up under Unfiled — nothing is ever silently lost.

7 The accountant's view

"And it all lands in the tool accountants already live in — Excel. Status, dates received, and plain-English notes you can forward to the client verbatim."

Click Open Manifest in Excel. Show the Status and Notes columns and the Unfiled sheet.

8 The trust close

"Three guarantees: every decision is a deterministic rule — no AI ever reads your tax documents. The scanner is read-only — it never moves, renames, or deletes a file. And your judgment always wins — one column in Excel overrides any automated result."

Point at the assurance bar at the bottom of this screen.
