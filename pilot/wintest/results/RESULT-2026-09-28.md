## Failures and notes

1. **Terms and tour reappear after a restart (step 16).**
   - **Expected:** once accepted, neither the terms nor the tour shows again on the next launch.
   - **What happened:** both came back.
   - **Evidence:**
     - `%APPDATA%\Tax Document Tracker Pilot\Local Storage\leveldb\000003.log` holds `pilot.terms.accepted = 1` and `pilot.tour.seen = 1` (written 22:18:31, when the earlier session closed). `PILOT.terms.version` is `1`, so the check in `app/renderer/pilot.js:78` should have matched.
     - The storage engine's `LOG` shows the database opened at 21:13:16 and 22:01:46, but **never for the launch at 22:18:36**. Nothing was written in that session either: after it closed, `000003.log` was unchanged at 22:18:31 and `LOG` still read 22:01:46.
     - So the restarted window apparently never reached its local storage. `readStored` fell to its "show the terms again" default, and the tour followed.
   - **Cause:** not found in this session (record, not fix). Possibly the new instance started while the previous one still held the storage, or storage was unavailable to that window.
   - **Screenshot:** none; Jason reported it verbally.
2. **The pilot's scheduled task disappeared before the uninstall.**
   - **Expected:** the task registered at step 14 is still there until the uninstall removes it.
   - **What happened:** `schtasks /XML` showed it at step 14 (about 22:17), but `uninstall_checks.ps1` (line 26, a plain `schtasks /Query /TN`) found it gone at 22:27. In between, the app was closed, restarted (Schedule still showed On) and closed again. After the uninstall, no task named "Tax Document Tracker Pilot" exists under any path.
   - **Where the removal can come from:** in `tracker/after_install.py`, only `_off()` (line 407) and the `ELSEWHERE` branch (line 450) call `scheduling.remove_task()`.
   - **The PC-name mismatch is ruled out** (checked at Jason's request):
     - `J Park & Associates\_Scheduling computer.txt` in the sample root names `jppc`.
     - `locking.this_host()` uses `platform.node()`. On this PC, `platform.node()`, `socket.gethostname()`, `COMPUTERNAME`, `hostname`, the registry's active and saved ComputerName, and the TCP/IP Hostname and NV Hostname all read `JPPC`.
     - The comparison casefolds, so this computer is the designated one and `ELSEWHERE` should not fire.
   - **Remaining suspect:** the `_off()` path. For example, a launch that read the schedule preference as off or absent, possibly the same restart that could not reach its storage (failure 1).
   - **No Task Scheduler history:** the event log is empty on this PC, so the exact moment is unknown.
   - **The real product's task "Tax Document Tracker" was not touched:** it still points at the real program and keeps its own schedule.
3. **Test suite:** one test-only failure, fixed in 588bc3b (see above). The installer is unaffected; per the orchestrator it was not rebuilt.
4. **Note:** `tracker-errors.log` has a WARNING from `validators: macro check (Mortgage Notes.docx)`: `zipfile.BadZipFile`. The made-up .docx is not a real zip. It was still routed to Needs Review as expected.
